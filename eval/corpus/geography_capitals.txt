National capitals are the cities where a country's government is officially based, and they are not always a country's largest or best-known city.

Paris is the capital of France. It has been the country's political center for centuries and is the seat of the French government.

Tokyo is the capital of Japan. It is one of the most populous metropolitan areas in the world and has served as the seat of the Japanese government since 1868.

Canberra is the capital of Australia, not Sydney. Canberra was purpose-built as a compromise capital between Sydney and Melbourne and became the seat of government in 1927.

Cairo is the capital of Egypt. It sits on the Nile River and is the largest city in the Arab world.

Ottawa is the capital of Canada, not Toronto. Ottawa was selected as the capital in 1857, in part because of its location on the border between English-speaking and French-speaking regions.
