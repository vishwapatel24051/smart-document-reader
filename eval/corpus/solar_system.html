The Solar System

The solar system consists of the Sun and everything that orbits it, including eight planets. Planets are grouped into inner (rocky) planets and outer (giant) planets based on their composition and distance from the Sun.

Inner Planets

The four inner planets are Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars. They are called rocky or terrestrial planets because they have solid surfaces made mostly of rock and metal.

Outer Planets

The four outer planets are Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. Jupiter and Saturn are called gas giants, while Uranus and Neptune are called ice giants because a larger portion of their mass is made up of heavier volatile substances.
