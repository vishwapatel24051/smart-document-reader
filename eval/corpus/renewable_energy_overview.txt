Renewable energy comes from sources that naturally replenish, unlike fossil fuels such as coal and oil.

Solar power generates electricity using photovoltaic panels, which convert sunlight directly into electricity. Solar panels contain semiconductor cells that release electrons when struck by photons of light.

Wind power generates electricity using turbines. Wind turns large blades connected to a generator, converting the kinetic energy of moving air into electrical energy.

Hydroelectric power generates electricity by directing flowing or falling water through turbines. Most hydroelectric power comes from dams that create a large reservoir of water at height, which is released through turbines to generate electricity on demand.

Of these three sources, hydroelectric power has historically supplied the largest share of renewable electricity generation worldwide, though solar and wind capacity have both grown rapidly in recent years.
