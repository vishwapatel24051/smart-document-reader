World War II: Key Dates

World War II was a global conflict that lasted from 1939 to 1945. The table below lists a small number of widely-recognized key dates from the war; it is not a complete timeline.

Table 1: Selected key dates of World War II
DateEvent
September 1, 1939Germany invades Poland; Britain and France declare war on Germany
December 7, 1941Japan attacks Pearl Harbor; the United States enters the war
June 6, 1944D-Day: Allied forces land in Normandy, France
May 8, 1945V-E Day: Germany surrenders, ending the war in Europe
